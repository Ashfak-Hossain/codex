**In plain words:** An ACK (acknowledgement) confirms receipt. In TCP, the acknowledgement number tells the sender which byte the receiver expects next. In DHCP, the final message of the exchange is also called an ACK.

**Think of it like this:** The signed receipt that comes back for a numbered box.
