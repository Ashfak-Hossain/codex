**In plain words:** ALPN (Application-Layer Protocol Negotiation) is a TLS extension where the client lists the protocols it speaks, such as `h2` and `http/1.1`, and the server picks one. It's defined in RFC 7301.

**Think of it like this:** Agreeing on a language before you open the box: "I can read English or Bangla." "English, then."
