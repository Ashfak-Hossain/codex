title: ARP spoofing
description: "An attack that sends fake ARP replies so traffic meant for one machine goes to the attacker."

date: 2026-11-23

**In plain words:** ARP spoofing, or ARP poisoning, sends forged ARP replies that tie someone else's IP address, often the gateway's, to the attacker's MAC address. Because ARP has no authentication, victims believe it and send their traffic to the attacker.

**Think of it like this:** Someone putting a fake name tag on their own door, so your neighbour's post gets delivered to them.
