**In plain words:** BOOTP (Bootstrap Protocol) is the older protocol DHCP grew out of. DHCP keeps BOOTP's message format and adds options, which is why DHCP messages carry fields like `yiaddr` and `giaddr`.

**Think of it like this:** The old paper form the new system still prints, with a few extra boxes added.
