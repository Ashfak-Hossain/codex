---
title: Broadcast
description: "A message sent to every machine on the local network at once."
tags:
  - glossary
date: 2026-11-23
---

**In plain words:** A broadcast is sent to every machine on the local network. At the link layer it goes to MAC address `ff:ff:ff:ff:ff:ff`; IPv4's own broadcast address is `255.255.255.255`. Routers don't forward broadcasts, so they stay on one network.

**Think of it like this:** Shouting a question down the street. Everyone on the street hears it, but nobody in the next town does.

**Learn it properly in** [[DHCP]] · [[ARP]].
