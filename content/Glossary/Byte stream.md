title: Byte stream

date: 2026-11-23
---

**In plain words:** A byte stream is an ordered flow of bytes with no edges marked in it. TCP delivers exactly the bytes that were sent, in order, but doesn't say where one message ends and the next begins.

**Think of it like this:** A long paper tape: every character arrives in order, but nobody marked where one letter stops and the next starts.
