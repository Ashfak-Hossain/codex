**In plain words:** A CDN (content delivery network) runs servers in many cities and caches a site's content on them, so readers get it from somewhere nearby. That cuts round-trip time and spreads the load.

**Think of it like this:** Local warehouses that stock popular items, so orders don't have to cross the country.
