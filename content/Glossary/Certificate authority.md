**In plain words:** A certificate authority (CA) checks that someone controls a domain and then signs a certificate for it. Browsers and operating systems ship with a list of CAs they trust; Let's Encrypt is one.

**Think of it like this:** The office that issues the ID cards. If you trust the office, you trust the cards it signs.
