**In plain words:** A certificate binds a domain name, such as `ashfak.dev`, to a public key, and is signed by a certificate authority. During the TLS handshake the server presents it, and the browser checks the signature and the name.

**Think of it like this:** An ID card issued by an office everyone trusts: it says "this seal belongs to this sender".
