**In plain words:** Connection refused means the destination answered straight away and said no, usually with a TCP reset because no program listens on that port. It fails fast, and it proves the network path works.

**Think of it like this:** Ringing a doorbell and someone shouting "nobody by that name here". Annoying, but at least you reached the house.
