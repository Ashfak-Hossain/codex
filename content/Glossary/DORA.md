**In plain words:** DORA names the four messages of a DHCP exchange: the client broadcasts a Discover, servers reply with an Offer, the client broadcasts a Request for one offer, and that server confirms with an Acknowledge.

**Think of it like this:** "Anyone have an address for me?" "Try this one." "I'll take yours, please." "It's yours."
