**In plain words:** The default gateway is the router your machine hands a packet to when the destination isn't on your own network. Your machine only needs to know this one exit; the gateway works out the rest.

**Think of it like this:** The local post office. Anything not for your own street goes there, and they handle the rest of the journey.

**Learn it properly in** [[ARP]] · [[DHCP]].
