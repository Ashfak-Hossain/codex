title: Dynamic ARP Inspection

**In plain words:** Dynamic ARP Inspection (DAI) checks every ARP message against the DHCP snooping table and drops any whose IP and MAC address don't match. That stops ARP spoofing on the switch.

**Think of it like this:** The front desk checking every "I live at number 7" claim against its own register before passing it on.

**Learn it properly in** [[ARP]] · [[DHCP]].
