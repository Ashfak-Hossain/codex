**In plain words:** Encapsulation means each layer wraps what it gets from above in its own header before passing it down. On the receiving side each layer removes its own header and passes the rest up.

**Think of it like this:** A letter in an envelope, in a box, in a sack: each wrapping is added by someone different, and each is removed by their counterpart at the other end.
