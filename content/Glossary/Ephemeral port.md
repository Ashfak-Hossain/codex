**In plain words:** An ephemeral port is a short-lived port number your operating system picks for your end of a connection, from a high range. The official range is 49152 to 65535 (RFC 6335); Linux uses 32768 to 60999 by default.

**Think of it like this:** A temporary return flat number written on your parcel, so the reply finds its way back to you.
