title: Frame

---

**In plain words:** A frame is what travels over one link, such as Wi-Fi or Ethernet. It wraps an IP packet with a link header holding the sender's and next machine's MAC addresses, and is rebuilt at every hop.

**Think of it like this:** The van's loading slip for one leg of the trip. At each post office the old slip is thrown away and a new one is written for the next van.
