**In plain words:** Framing is how an application marks message boundaries in a byte stream, for example by sending each message's length first, or ending each one with a special character such as a newline.

**Think of it like this:** Writing "letter 2, 340 words" at the top of each letter on the paper tape, so the reader knows where to cut.
