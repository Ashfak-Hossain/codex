**In plain words:** A gratuitous ARP is an announcement a machine makes about its own IP and MAC address without anyone asking. It updates other machines' caches, for example after a failover, and helps spot two machines using the same IP address.

**Think of it like this:** Telling the whole street "I've moved in at number 7" before anyone asks.
