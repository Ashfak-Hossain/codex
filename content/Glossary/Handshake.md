**In plain words:** A handshake is the opening exchange that sets up a connection. TCP's takes three messages (SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK); TLS adds its own on top to agree on keys and check the certificate.

**Think of it like this:** Phoning ahead before a delivery: "Are you there?" "Yes." "Sending now."
