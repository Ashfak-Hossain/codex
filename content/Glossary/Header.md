**In plain words:** A header is a small, fixed-format block a layer adds in front of the data it carries. It holds what that layer needs, such as addresses, port numbers, lengths or sequence numbers.

**Think of it like this:** The label stuck on the outside of each wrapping: it tells the person handling that wrapping what to do, without opening it.
