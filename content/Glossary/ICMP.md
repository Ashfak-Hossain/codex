**In plain words:** ICMP (Internet Control Message Protocol) carries IP's error and diagnostic messages, such as "destination unreachable" or "time exceeded". `ping` uses ICMP echo requests and replies. It's defined in RFC 792.

**Think of it like this:** The post office's little notes: "address unknown" or "returned to sender".
