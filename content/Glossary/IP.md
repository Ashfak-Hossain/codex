**In plain words:** IP (Internet Protocol) carries packets across networks from a source address to a destination address. It does its best but promises nothing: packets can be lost, duplicated or reordered, and the layer above has to cope. IPv4 is RFC 791; IPv6 is RFC 8200.

**Think of it like this:** The postal service's address system: it moves labelled boxes between cities, but doesn't track or insure them.
