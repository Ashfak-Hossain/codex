**In plain words:** A layer is one level of the networking stack. It does one job, such as finding the next machine or putting bytes in order, and trusts the layer below it to do the rest.

**Think of it like this:** Sending a parcel takes a writer, a packer, a labeller and a driver. Each does one job and never needs to know how the others do theirs.
