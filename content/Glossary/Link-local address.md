**In plain words:** A link-local address is one a machine picks for itself when nothing gives it one. IPv4 link-local addresses start with `169.254` (RFC 3927). Seeing one usually means DHCP failed, and the machine can only reach neighbours on the same link.

**Think of it like this:** Writing a made-up house number on your door because the council never sent one. Your neighbours can find you, but the post office can't.
