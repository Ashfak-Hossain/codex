**In plain words:** A load balancer sits in front of several servers and shares the work between them. A layer 4 load balancer chooses by IP address and port without reading the data; a layer 7 one reads the HTTP request and can choose by path or header.

**Think of it like this:** A sorting desk that sends each customer to the shortest queue, either by ticket number alone, or after reading what they've come for.
