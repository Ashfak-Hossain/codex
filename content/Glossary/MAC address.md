**In plain words:** A MAC (media access control) address is a 48-bit link-layer address, written as six pairs of hex digits, such as `3c:22:fb:5a:10:9e`. It only matters on the local network: it gets a frame to the next machine, not to the final destination.

**Think of it like this:** The name on a house's front door. The van driver needs it for the last few metres, but it means nothing to a post office in another city.
