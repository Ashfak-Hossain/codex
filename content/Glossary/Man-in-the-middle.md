---
title: Man-in-the-middle
description: "An attacker who secretly sits between two parties and can read or change what they send."
tags:
  - glossary
aliases:
  - MITM
date: 2026-11-23
---

**In plain words:** In a man-in-the-middle attack, someone sits between two parties who think they're talking directly, and relays, reads or alters their traffic. TLS defends against it by checking the server's certificate.

**Think of it like this:** A crooked clerk who opens every box, reads the letter, reseals it and sends it on.

**Learn it properly in** [[ARP]] · [[DHCP]].
