**In plain words:** NAT (network address translation) lets many devices on a private network share one public IPv4 address. The router rewrites each outgoing packet's source address and port, and remembers the change so replies find their way back. A full page on NAT is planned.

**Think of it like this:** An office where all outgoing mail gets the company's street address, and a clerk keeps a list of who sent what so replies reach the right desk.
