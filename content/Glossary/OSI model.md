**In plain words:** The OSI (Open Systems Interconnection) model splits networking into seven layers: physical, data link, network, transport, session, presentation and application. It's a teaching reference; the internet's own protocols fit a simpler model with fewer layers.

**Think of it like this:** A detailed official chart of every job in the postal service. Useful for talking about the jobs, even though real post offices merge some of them.
