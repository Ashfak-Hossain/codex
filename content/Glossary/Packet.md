**In plain words:** A packet is the network layer's unit: your data with an IP header in front, holding the source and destination IP addresses. Routers forward it hop by hop, and the destination address doesn't change on the way.

**Think of it like this:** A box with its address label. The label stays the same from the first post office to the last, however many vans carry it.
