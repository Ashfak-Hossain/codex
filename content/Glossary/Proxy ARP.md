---
title: Proxy ARP
description: "A router answering ARP questions on behalf of machines on another network."
tags:
  - glossary
date: 2026-11-23
---

**In plain words:** With proxy ARP, a router replies to ARP requests for addresses that are actually on another network, giving its own MAC address. Traffic then flows through the router without the sender knowing.

**Think of it like this:** A receptionist who answers "yes, that's me" for anyone in the building, and passes the post along.
