**In plain words:** QUIC is a transport protocol that runs over UDP and builds in TLS 1.3, so a new connection needs fewer round trips than TCP plus TLS. HTTP/3 runs on QUIC. It's defined in RFC 9000.

**Think of it like this:** A courier who checks your ID and takes your first parcel in the same visit, instead of making two trips.
