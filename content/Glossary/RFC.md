**In plain words:** An RFC (Request for Comments) is a numbered document published by the IETF and others. Many RFCs define internet protocols exactly, such as RFC 791 for IPv4 or RFC 9293 for TCP, and an RFC never changes once published.

**Think of it like this:** The rule book of the postal service, one numbered chapter at a time.
