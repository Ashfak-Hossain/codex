**In plain words:** A round trip is one message out and its reply back; the round-trip time (RTT) is how long that takes. It's limited by distance: light in fibre covers about 200 km per millisecond, so far-away servers always cost more round-trip time.

**Think of it like this:** Posting a letter and waiting for the reply. Nothing you do can make the post travel faster than the roads allow.
