title: Router

tags:

date: 2026-11-23
---

**In plain words:** A router connects networks and forwards packets between them. For each packet it reads the destination IP address, looks it up in its routing table, and sends the packet to the next hop.

**Think of it like this:** A sorting office: it reads the address label, puts the box on the right outgoing van, and never opens the box.
