**In plain words:** In this notebook's five-layer model, the security layer sits directly under the application. It encrypts what the application sends and checks that the server is who it claims to be. In practice this is TLS.

**Think of it like this:** The letter goes into a locked box. Only the person with the key can open it, and the box comes with a seal that proves who sent it.
