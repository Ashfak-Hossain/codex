**In plain words:** A segment is TCP's unit: a slice of the byte stream with a TCP header in front, holding the source and destination ports, a sequence number and an acknowledgement number.

**Think of it like this:** One of the numbered boxes. The number lets the receiver put the boxes back in order and notice a gap.
