---
title: Switch
description: "A device that connects machines in one network and forwards frames by MAC address."
tags:
  - glossary
date: 2026-11-23
---

**In plain words:** A switch connects machines within one local network and forwards each frame only to the port where the destination MAC address lives. It learns which MAC address is on which port by watching traffic.

**Think of it like this:** The internal mail room of one building: it knows which desk is where, but never deals with other cities.

**Learn it properly in** [[DHCP]] · [[ARP]].
