**In plain words:** TLS (Transport Layer Security) encrypts what two programs send each other and lets the client check the server's identity through its certificate. The current version, TLS 1.3, is RFC 8446.

**Think of it like this:** The locked box with a tamper-proof seal: nobody on the way can read the letter, and the seal proves who packed it.
