**In plain words:** Timed out means no reply came back within the waiting time. The packets were lost or silently dropped somewhere, often by a firewall, or the machine is off. It fails slowly, and it tells you less than a refusal.

**Think of it like this:** Ringing the doorbell and hearing nothing at all. You can't tell if nobody's home or the bell is broken.
