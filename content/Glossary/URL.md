**In plain words:** A URL (uniform resource locator) names a resource on the web. In `https://ashfak.dev/notes`, `https` is the scheme (how to fetch it), `ashfak.dev` is the host, and `/notes` is the path on that host.

**Think of it like this:** A full delivery instruction: "by courier, to this building, to this flat".
