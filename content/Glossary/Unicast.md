---
title: Unicast
description: "A message sent from one machine to exactly one other machine."
tags:
  - glossary
date: 2026-11-23
---

**In plain words:** Unicast means one sender and one receiver: the message is addressed to a single machine. Almost all traffic is unicast.

**Think of it like this:** A parcel addressed to one person, as opposed to a flyer pushed through every door.

**Learn it properly in** [[DHCP]] · [[ARP]].
