# Combinatorics

Counting things too numerous to list. The chapter starts from the two rules every counting argument is built on and grows toward the standard competitive-programming toolkit. Read in order — each page leans on the one before it.

1. [[Counting Principles]] — the rule of product (AND) and rule of sum (OR), counting by bijection, and dividing away overcounting.
2. [[Permutations and Combinations]] — arranging versus choosing: $nPr$, $nCr$, and the with/without-repetition variants.
3. [[Binomial Coefficients]] — Pascal's triangle, the binomial theorem, and the core identities (hockey-stick, Vandermonde).
4. [[Inclusion–Exclusion]] — counting overlapping sets, with derangements as the classic application.

_Builds on the counting intuition only — no prior chapter required._
