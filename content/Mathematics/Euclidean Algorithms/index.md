# Euclidean Algorithms

Divisibility and the greatest common divisor — Euclid's $2300$-year-old method and the one extra step that turns it into the engine behind modular inverses.

1. [[Greatest Common Divisor]] — computing $\gcd(a,b)$ by the modulo recurrence in $O(\log n)$, with Lamé's Fibonacci worst case.
2. [[Extended Euclidean Algorithm]] — the same recursion carrying two coefficients, returning $x, y$ with $a x + b y = \gcd(a,b)$ (Bézout's identity).
