# Primes

Primes are the atoms of multiplication. This chapter covers finding them in bulk, testing a single number, breaking a number apart, and the multiplicative function that counts coprimes.

1. [[Sieve of Eratosthenes]] — every prime up to $N$ by crossing out composites, in $O(N \log\log N)$.
2. [[Primality Tests]] — is one number prime? From trial division to deterministic Miller–Rabin.
3. [[Prime Factorization]] — breaking $n$ into primes: trial division and a smallest-prime-factor sieve.
4. [[Euler's Totient Function]] — $\varphi(n)$, counting integers coprime to $n$; Euler's theorem and its reach.

_Builds on the [[Modular Arithmetic]] chapter._
